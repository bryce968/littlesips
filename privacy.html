Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 (iCloud sync)

In short

Little Sips does not collect any personal data. What you log is stored on your iPhone and, for syncing, in your own private iCloud. We never have access to it.

What the app stores

Feeds (times, durations, sides), bottle feeds (time and amount), the running timer and your settings are saved on your device.

iCloud sync and sharing

If you are signed in to iCloud, your log is also stored in your own private iCloud using Apple's CloudKit, so it is backed up and can sync between devices. This data is protected by your Apple Account and handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We (the developer) cannot see or access it.

If you choose Share with partner, only the people you invite can see and edit the shared log. You can stop sharing or leave a shared log at any time.

What the app does not do

No separate account or sign-in. No analytics or tracking. No advertising. No third-party services or SDKs – only Apple's own iCloud. No location, contacts, photos or health data.

Notifications and Live Activities

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local reminder on your iPhone when a feed runs unusually long. The lock screen timer (Live Activity) is also created locally. Neither sends any data off your device.

Children

The app is intended for parents and caregivers. It does not collect information from or about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the change takes effect.
