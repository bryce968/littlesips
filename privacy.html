Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

In short

Little Sips does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Feeds (times, durations, sides), bottle feeds (time and amount) and your settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you use iCloud Backup, your iPhone may include this data in your personal backup, which is managed by Apple.

What the app does not do

No account or sign-in. No analytics or tracking. No advertising. No third-party services or SDKs. No location, contacts, photos or health data.

Notifications and Live Activities

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local reminder on your iPhone when a feed runs unusually long. The lock screen timer (Live Activity) is also created locally. Neither sends any data off your device.

Children

The app is intended for parents and caregivers. It does not collect information from or about children.

Changes

If this policy changes – for example if an optional sync feature is added – this page will be updated before the change takes effect.
