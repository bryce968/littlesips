Frequently asked questions

How do I pause for a burp break?

Tap the big button while a feed is running. You then have a few minutes to tap Continue and the same feed carries on. You can change how long in Settings.

How do I finish a feed?

Press and hold the big button until the ring around it is full. You can also tap Finish on the lock screen.

I logged the wrong side or forgot to stop. Can I fix it?

Yes. Tap any entry in the log or in History to change the time, the side or the amount. You can also delete it there.

Which side does the app suggest next?

The side you did not start with last time. You can always tap Left or Right to choose yourself.

The timer doesn't show on my lock screen.

Open the iPhone Settings, find the app, and make sure Live Activities is turned on.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.
